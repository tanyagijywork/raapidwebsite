Type scale · Major Third (1.25)

Base 16px × 1.25 per step. Each size is the previous one × 1.25, rounded to the nearest 0.5px. Sizes are in rem (1rem = 16px) so they respect user font settings. The three largest steps are fluid and scale down on small screens.

Readability & accessibility rules

No thin weights on small textBody, captions and labels use 400 or 500. Light (300) only from 39px up; ExtraLight (200) only at Display size and on light backgrounds.
Line heightBody copy 1.5–1.6 (meets WCAG 1.4.12 text spacing). Headings tighten as they grow: 1.4 down to 1.05.
Line length45–75 characters per line (about 60ch) for paragraphs.
TrackingSlightly negative on large headings (down to −0.03em), slightly positive on uppercase labels (+0.06em).
Minimum sizeNever below 12.8px (0.8rem). Interactive text and form inputs stay at 16px or more.
EmphasisUse weight 500, not 200 or 300, to emphasise. Do not rely on colour alone.
ContrastText meets 4.5:1 (3:1 for 24px+ / 19px bold). See the Colour tab for approved pairs.
Dark backgroundsLight text on dark appears thinner. Step the weight up one level (300 → 400) below 61px.

Accessibility · WebAIM contrast

Signal Green is not accessible on a white background. Always check the contrast ratio before pairing colours.

Ratios are calculated from the hex values (WCAG 2.x). AA needs 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text.
